# Future Planning (48-month horizon)

## Why level matters most

The ending evaluation covers career level, total wealth, health and dignity,
skill, risk control and network. The published salary table rises steeply
with level, and half-year bonuses are multiples of the monthly base. So each
promotion raises both the level dimension and every later month's wealth.
**Earlier promotions are worth more than later ones.** The plan should aim to
pass as many six-month promotion windows as possible.

## Objective: pass every window

Treat every promotion window as one to win. Never treat the current level as
good enough, and never coast after a promotion: the next window starts the
moment the last one closes, and its bar is higher.

- A promotion improves two ending dimensions at once (career level, and
  total wealth through the higher salary and bonuses). So, after survival,
  promotion is the first objective. During the run, Health and Dignity are
  held in the target band set in `adaptive-decision-planning` (upper-middle
  of the scale), not kept full; anything above the band is a budget to
  spend on Output and Network.
- The run is scored as an average over several runs, and an early exit
  ruins a run. Push hard, but never into a failure direction: a gamble that
  risks ending the run is never worth one extra level. Keeping hidden risk
  defused comes before the push for a window: a blow-up can wipe out half
  a year of Output, which costs the window anyway.
- If a window is missed, find which promotion input is behind (from the
  stats and the manager feedback) and put most of the next half-year into
  it.

## The six-month cycle

Every six months there is a performance review and a promotion window.
Promotion considers Skill, Network and Output, rewards sustained results, and
requirements rise with level. Plan each half-year as one unit:

- **First quarter of the half:** build. Defuse risk if the risk mode is
  raised, repair any stat below its band, then grow Output and Network.
- **Second quarter of the half (review is next):** show results. Weight Output
  and visible delivery higher in event choices, Energy and the main action.
  Avoid choices that cost Output or Network.
- **After the review:** read the grade and the manager feedback (see
  `feedback-assessment`) and set the focus for the next half.

## After a promotion

A new level brings higher expectations. Expect Network friction after a fast
climb. Spend early effort in the new level on Network and on keeping Output
growing, so the next window is reachable. Because each level's bar is
higher, growth per half-year must speed up as the level rises: from the
middle levels on, use every Energy point and main action on Output and
Network, after Defuse when the risk mode is raised.

## Phase guidance

- **Year 1:** build Output and Network, keep Health and Dignity in their
  band, and keep risk defused. Early stats may be held at the starting
  level until the first promotion; clean event handling matters most then.
- **Years 2 and 3:** this is where most promotions are won or lost. Keep
  Output and Network growing alongside Skill. A stat parked at its cap is
  spare capacity to convert into Output.
- **Year 4:** keep pushing for the remaining promotion windows, including
  the last one at the end of the run. In the final quarter, use Energy on
  Health recovery and Mental recovery to bring both back towards the top of
  the scale, while event choices and the main action still push the
  promotion inputs. The ending evaluates the final condition, so Health,
  Dignity, Network and Wealth must end in a healthy state. Do not take new
  risks whose consequences would land after the run ends without benefit.

## Wealth

Wealth comes mainly from salary and review bonuses, so level and review
grades drive it. Avoid choices that cost Wealth unless they prevent a larger
loss, and never let Wealth stay negative.
